# Product

## Register

both

## Users

Music listeners discovering, curating, and purchasing music directly from artists. They seek rare, exclusive tracks with a sense of luxury and exclusivity—not algorithmic curation, but editorial discovery. They value artists and want the listening experience to feel crafted and premium.

## Product Purpose

Democratize music distribution by creating a direct-to-consumer marketplace where artists retain creative control and revenue, while listeners discover music through a carefully curated, luxury-feeling platform. Success is measured by artist empowerment, discovery quality, and the sense of exclusivity users feel when engaging with the platform.

## Brand Personality

Futuristic, tech-minimalist, sophisticated. The interface whispers rather than shouts. Minimalist luxury—every pixel intentional, nothing excess, nothing cheap. The platform should feel like you've been given access to something exclusive and carefully curated, not a generic music store.

## Anti-references

- Algorithmic chaos (avoid Spotify's overwhelming personalization or TikTok's endless scroll)
- Cluttered dashboards (no visual noise, no sidebar overload)
- Corporate blandness (Tidal, Apple Music templates)
- AI-generated look (avoid cheap gradients, generic animations, or web3/crypto aesthetics)

## Design Principles

1. **Exclusivity over accessibility** (not WCAG, but gatekeeping via craft): the interface should feel special because it's so well-made, not because it's open to everyone
2. **Artist-first**: the artist's vision and work is the hero; interface is the stage
3. **Minimalist luxury**: restrained palette, expansive whitespace, deliberate typography—every element earns its place
4. **Innovative constraint**: push boundaries within a tight visual language, not by adding more
5. **Handmade over automated**: the platform should feel curated, not algorithmically generated

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Focus on the **feel** of craftsmanship and exclusivity. While basic accessibility (keyboard nav, color contrast) should be solid, prioritize the premium, curated aesthetic over WCAG level. The goal is to make users feel like they've been admitted to an exclusive community, not accommodated by a generic platform.
